# About

## Maintenance

The FRAME Architecture is maintained and developed by the **FRAME Maintainers**, a group of organisations that took over the responsibility of the FRAME Forum for the European ITS Framework Architecture after the end of the FRAME-NEXT project.

 - the FRAME Architecture Tool and its history: [github.com/FRAME-ITS/FRAME](https://github.com/FRAME-ITS/FRAME)
 - this website: [github.com/FRAME-ITS/FRAME-online](https://github.com/FRAME-ITS/FRAME-online)
 - questions, problems and suggestions: [GitHub issues](https://github.com/FRAME-ITS/FRAME/issues) or contact@frame-online.eu
