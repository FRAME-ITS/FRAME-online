# Articles & related Reports

## Papers

[Leveraging FRAME Methodology for EU-Wide Harmonization of National Access Points (Transactions on Transport Sciences, 2025)](https://www.tots.upol.cz/artkey/tot-202503-0004_leveraging-frame-methodology-for-eu-wide-harmonization-of-national-access-points.php)

How the FRAME views were used to build the NAPCORE NAP Reference Architecture, and how the views build on each other.

[Using the FRAME Architecture for Planning Integrated Intelligent Transport Systems (EATIS 2009)](../assets/docs/articles/eatis-2009.pdf) · [ITS Spain 2009 version](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5973610)

Why ITS needs an architecture-based systems engineering approach, and how the FRAME Architecture and its methodology support it; extension to cooperative systems.

[Using the European ITS Framework Architecture (ITS World Congress 2006)](../assets/docs/articles/its-wc-2006.pdf)

A regional ITS architecture created with FRAME and the Browsing and Selection Tools, from stakeholder aspirations to physical links, with an evaluation of the result.

[Different Types of ITS Architectures and their Uses (ITS World Congress 2005)](../assets/docs/articles/its-wc-2005.pdf)

Framework, Defined and Specific ITS architectures: what each contains and when to use which.

[How Can an ITS Architecture be Created – A European View (ITS World Congress 2004)](../assets/docs/articles/its-wc-2004-bossom.pdf)

Overview of the architecture creation process, the role of stakeholders and the use of the results in deployment, including multi-modal architectures.

[Why Do You Need an ITS Architecture – European and National Perspectives (ITS World Congress 2004)](../assets/docs/articles/its-wc-2004-jesty.pdf)

The benefits of an ITS architecture for stakeholders, and why deployments without one become "technology islands".

[Configuration Management in the European ITS Framework Architecture Environment (ITS World Congress 2003)](../assets/docs/articles/its-wc-2003-bossom.pdf)

Configuration management practices for the maintainers and the users of the Framework Architecture.

[Involving Stakeholders in ITS Architecture Creation (ITS World Congress 2003)](../assets/docs/articles/its-wc-2003-jesty.pdf)

How to capture stakeholder aspirations and turn them into User Needs.

[FRAME Navigation Tool for Easy Use of the European ITS Framework Architecture](../assets/docs/articles/TS23%20Nigro%20Navig%20tool.pdf)

The FRAME Browsing Tool developed by the FRAME-S project.

[Deployment of European ITS Framework Architecture (IEEE ITSC 2001)](../assets/docs/articles/IEEE-ITSC2001.DOC) · [Architecture Experiences in the Urban Environment (2000)](../assets/docs/articles/EU-ITS2000.DOC)

Early national and urban use of the first version of the Framework Architecture.

## Thinking Highways series on E-FRAME (2010–2011)

 - [A Particularly Common Goal](../assets/docs/articles/A-Particularly-Common-Goal-TH-Nov-Dec-2010.pdf) (Vol 5 No 4, Nov/Dec 2010) – how FRAME supports the ITS Action Plan and the ITS Directive.
